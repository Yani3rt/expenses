# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Household members reviewing spending together in a low-stress domestic context. They want to understand recent expenses, search for a transaction quickly, compare categories and periods, and confirm that the underlying data is current without navigating a professional accounting tool.

## Product Purpose

Expense Viewer is a read-only window into the canonical expense-tracker database. It should make spending easy to scan and questions easy to answer while clearly communicating data freshness and the app's non-editing posture. Success means a user can understand the household's current spending and find a specific expense within seconds.

## Brand Personality

Playful, clear, and reassuring. The product should feel like a friendly household cockpit: trustworthy and polished, with small moments of delight that never compete with the data.

## Anti-references

- Dense professional finance terminals or spreadsheet-first accounting software.
- Generic AI-generated fintech dashboards built from interchangeable metric cards, glass panels, and decorative gradients.
- Interfaces that imply expenses, budgets, or source data can be edited from this read-only viewer.
- Choreographed or slow product motion that delays access to the task.

## Design Principles

- Make the answer obvious before exposing supporting detail.
- Optimize for fast search, clear comparison, and readable spending overviews.
- Preserve trust by making read-only status and data freshness unambiguous.
- Use household-friendly language instead of accounting jargon.
- Earn delight through quick, purposeful responses rather than decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal accessibility conformance target is currently required. Preserve basic semantic HTML, keyboard operability, legible contrast, and reduced-motion safeguards where they are already present or inexpensive to maintain, but do not prioritize a dedicated accessibility program in the current improvement scope.
