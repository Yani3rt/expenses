## Design Context

### Users
The expense viewer is for a household reviewing spending together. Users are checking categories, recent expenses, totals, and database freshness in a low-stress domestic context rather than a finance-professional environment.

### Brand Personality
Playful, clear, and reassuring. The product should feel like a friendly household cockpit: useful and trustworthy, but with small moments of delight.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light-mode dashboard with soft surfaces, category color accents, rounded forms, and polished micro-interactions. Motion should feel premium but not corporate: gentle entrances, responsive hover feedback, and subtle chart movement.

### Design Principles
- Keep the app calm and readable first; motion should support understanding, not distract.
- Add playful delight through small, quick responses on cards, nav, and charts.
- Use CSS-only transform and opacity animations for performance.
- Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Preserve household-friendly clarity over dense financial-tool complexity.
